# Paper prose

Read this for introductions, body text, abstracts, or any prose meant for a journal. The core rules in SKILL.md still apply.

## Shape

Build up to the conclusion. He sets context first and arrives at the claim. His introductions open wide ("Disputes, as well as agreements, are part of ordinary economic life") and walk down to the contribution over several sentences. Preserve this movement. Do not restructure into conclusion-first order unless he asks.

At the macro level, an introduction opens with real-world evidence or empirical motivation, then arrives at the theoretical question. Do not open with the contribution or the main result.

## Register

Careful and precise, but still in plain sentences. No contractions. Technical vocabulary is welcome; AI filler and connective-stacking are not. "Therefore" is fine when it does genuine causal work. He is comfortable being direct about a contribution ("In this study, we depart from the existing literature"). He uses semicolons freely. No exclamation marks, no irony.

## Habits to keep

**"In a nutshell, ..."** His compression phrase, used when reducing a complex argument to its essential logic before the technical detail. Slightly informal, efficient, and genuinely his.

**Labeled statements.** In empirical and experimental sections, "Observation 1," "Result 2" are a natural organizing device. Preserve them.
