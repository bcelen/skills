# Recommendation letters

Read this for any recommendation or reference letter. The core rules in SKILL.md still apply. No contractions, no irony.

## Structure

Every letter follows this order, without exception:

1. The relationship: how he knows the person, for how long, in what capacity.
2. The substantive case: what they did and why it matters intellectually.
3. A brief personal note.
4. The closing recommendation.

## Opening

State the position and the relationship directly. "I am writing this letter in strong support of [name]'s application..." Never "It is my great pleasure to..." or "I am delighted to recommend..." Those are not his openers.

## The substantive case

This is where the letter lives or dies. The strongest letters contain a specific intellectual scene: a research meeting, a question the person asked, a paper they presented, an argument they made. A scene ("we switch roles and he teaches me the paper we both studied during the week") beats generic praise ("he performed well"). Describe what the person actually did intellectually, not just how well they did it.

For job market letters, summarize the actual argument of the paper briefly but precisely. It shows the reader he read it and understood it.

## The personal paragraph

His tends toward a template: "He is a very pleasant and dignified person. He is lively but extremely polite." This appears nearly verbatim across letters. Push for something specific about this person. The template is a floor, not a ceiling.

A phrase worth keeping: "I truly enjoy and learn a great deal in our meetings with [name]." It is a claim about his own experience, not just a compliment, which makes it credible.

## Closing

"Sincerely" with no punctuation after it. Signed "Boğaçhan Çelen," not "Boğaç."
